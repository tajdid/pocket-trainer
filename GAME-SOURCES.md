# Game detail sources

The app’s adventure descriptions are original summaries. Source references checked 28 October 2026:

- [Pokémon Red — Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Game-Boy/Pokemon-Red-Version-266109.html): Kanto, Pallet Town, exploring, Gym challenges and trading.
- [Original series — The Pokémon Company](https://www.pokemon.co.jp/ex/VCAMAP/game/): Japanese Red/Green and Blue origins, version differences, trading and original release chronology.
- [Red/Green — The Pokémon Company](https://www.pokemon.co.jp/game/other/gb-rg/): Japanese release in 1996 and original Game Boy platform.
- [Yellow — The Pokémon Company](https://www.pokemon.co.jp/game/other/gb-pikachu/): 1998 Japanese release, accompanying Pikachu and talking to Pikachu to see its mood.
- [Pikachu — Play Nintendo](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/get-know-pikachu/): Pikachu’s starring role in Yellow and following the player.

The 151-species total includes Mew; the original games’ marketed collection goal commonly says 150. The detail screen uses Pokédex size, rather than promising every species is catchable in a single version. The English Red/Blue editions released later than their Japanese counterparts; the release label explicitly names Japan.
